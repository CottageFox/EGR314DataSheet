## Overview
Here is my power budget. I have the ESP32 on 3.3V, the Motor Driver on 3.3V and 12V, and I have the motor on 12V. As shown with the calculator, there is enough power to support my system.

I used my power budget to estimate my power needs based on the maximum power my system will need compared to how much I have. For my components I had around 350 mA of current left on my 3.3V line, so there was some room to add more if I needed to, but for my 12V line with my motor, I had only 46mA left with the safety margin to comfortably run my motor, so while I have enough power, it may be a struggle if everything runs at its max current load.
