## Overview
This is my schematic for team 301's project. It highlights 3 power options going into a fuse then power regulator, the Motor Driver and Motor I am using, and the ESP32 microcontroller I am using. I also have an LED and Button for debugging. There is a micro USB port to program the ESP32. I also have extra headers on every pin that is unused. There are 2 ribbon cable connectors that will connect to my teamates.
